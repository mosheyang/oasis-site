How do I add a passage?

Tap + on the Verses tab. Search a reference such as “Ps 23” or “1 Cor 13:4-7”, or browse to a book and chapter. Tap one verse, then another to select the range between them, or tap a paragraph chip to select a whole paragraph or stanza. Then tap Add and learn.

How do I use a Bible in another language?

On the add screen, tap the Bible button at the top right. Your iPhone’s languages are listed first; search or scroll for any other. Pick a language, then a version. After you select verses, the Bible button in the bottom bar shows the same verses in other versions so you can compare before adding.

Can I learn a passage in two languages?

Yes. Tap the passage on the Verses tab, then Add a language. Each language keeps its own review schedule. Choose your languages and default Bibles in Settings (the gear on the Progress tab).

Does it work offline?

The built-in Berean Standard Bible, King James Version, and World English Bible always work offline, and so do passages you’ve already added in any Bible. Public-domain Bibles can be downloaded from the Bible list. Other Bibles need an internet connection to browse and add new passages, as their publishers’ licenses require.

Why isn’t the ESV available?

Oasis offers the Bibles its publishers license through the YouVersion Platform. If a translation you want isn’t listed, its publisher hasn’t made it available there yet.

How do I remove a passage I added by mistake?

In practice, tap ••• and choose Remove from my verses. On the Verses tab, swipe left on the passage, or open it and choose Delete passage from •••.

The “Say” step doesn’t hear me.

Check that Oasis is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition in Settings → Oasis. Speak at a normal pace in a quiet place. If it loses its place, tap the microphone to start again. Some languages don’t have speech recognition on iPhone; for those, Oasis lets you say each phrase, then reveal it and mark whether you had it.

When will a passage come back?

After each session you rate how well you knew it. Oasis schedules the next review from that rating, spacing passages further apart as they take root. The Today tab shows everything that’s due.

Is my data backed up?

Your passages and progress are stored on your iPhone and included in your device’s iCloud or computer backup. Oasis does not have an account or server. See the privacy policy.
