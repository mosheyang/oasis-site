Privacy Policy

Oasis: Scripture Memory · Effective November 12, 2026

Oasis is built to keep your practice private. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What stays on your phone

The passages you add, your practice history, review schedule, and any memory palace pictures you write are saved only on your device. Oasis has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code that tracks you. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The “Say” step asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition so Oasis can follow along while you recite a passage. Recognition is done by Apple: on your device where Apple supports your passage’s language, and otherwise through Apple’s speech recognition service, in which case Oasis tells you before it listens. Apple’s handling of that audio is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. Oasis itself does not record, save, or send your audio or transcripts anywhere. You can turn these permissions off at any time in Settings, and every other part of the app keeps working.

Bible text

The Berean Standard Bible, King James Version, and World English Bible are built into the app and are in the public domain. Reading and practicing them involves no network request.

Other Bibles come from YouVersion’s official developer service, the YouVersion Platform (api.youversion.com). When you browse Bibles, read a chapter, add a passage, or download a Bible, Oasis asks YouVersion for that Bible text, its copyright notice, or the list of available Bibles. These requests contain the app’s key and the Bible or passage requested, and nothing about you: no account, device identifier, or list of your passages. Like any web request, they reach YouVersion’s servers from your internet connection. Text you add is saved with your passage on your device, so practice keeps working offline.

Verse of the day

To suggest the same verse of the day you see in the YouVersion Bible App, Oasis downloads that year’s list of verse references from the YouVersion Platform about once a year and keeps it on your device. The request contains no information about you.

Your languages

Oasis reads your iPhone’s preferred languages, on the device only, to suggest Bibles in those languages. Your language choices are saved on your device and never sent anywhere.

Children

Oasis does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
