Privacy Policy

Oasis: Scripture Memory · Effective November 10, 2026

Oasis is built to keep your practice private. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What stays on your phone

The passages you add, your practice history, review schedule, and any memory palace pictures you write are saved only on your device. Oasis has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code that tracks you. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The “Say” step asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition so Oasis can follow along while you recite a passage. Audio is processed by Apple’s speech recognition service, on your device where supported. Oasis does not record, save, or send your audio or transcripts anywhere. You can turn these permissions off at any time in Settings, and every other part of the app keeps working.

Bible text

The Berean Standard Bible, King James Version, and World English Bible are built into the app and are in the public domain. Reading and practicing them does not involve any network request.

Verse of the day

To suggest the same verse of the day you see in the YouVersion Bible App, Oasis downloads that year’s list of verse references from YouVersion (bible.com) about once a year and keeps it on your device. The request contains no account, identifier, or information about you or your passages; like any web request, it reaches YouVersion’s servers from your internet connection. The verse itself is shown from the translation built into Oasis.

Children

Oasis does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
